### Overall Evaluation

**1. Working Environment**

Throughout my internship, I experienced a professional, supportive, and welcoming working environment. Team members were always willing to share their knowledge and assist whenever I encountered technical difficulties. This atmosphere helped me adapt quickly and become more confident when working with new technologies and real-world projects. I believe that organizing more team-building or networking activities would further strengthen collaboration among interns and team members.

**2. Support from Mentor and Team**

My mentor provided valuable guidance throughout the internship by explaining concepts clearly, reviewing my work, and offering constructive feedback. Instead of simply providing solutions, my mentor encouraged me to research independently and develop my own problem-solving skills. The administrative team also ensured that all necessary documents and resources were available, making the internship process smooth and efficient.

**3. Relevance of the Internship to My Major**

The tasks assigned during the internship were closely related to my academic background in Software Engineering. I had the opportunity to apply my knowledge of web development while gaining practical experience with cloud computing technologies such as AWS. This allowed me to bridge the gap between theoretical knowledge and real-world software development.

**4. Learning and Professional Development**

The internship significantly enhanced both my technical and professional skills. I improved my knowledge of React, TypeScript, Spring Boot, RESTful APIs, AWS services, and cloud-based application development. In addition, I gained experience in project collaboration, source code management, technical documentation, and professional communication, all of which will be valuable in my future career.

**5. Company Culture and Teamwork**

The company promotes a positive and collaborative working culture. Team members treat each other with respect, communicate openly, and work together to solve problems efficiently. This supportive environment helped me integrate into the team and motivated me to contribute actively to the project despite being an intern.

**6. Internship Support**

The internship program provided a valuable learning experience by allowing interns to participate in real software development projects while receiving guidance from experienced mentors. Access to technical resources and continuous feedback created an excellent environment for improving both technical knowledge and soft skills.

### Additional Questions

**What satisfied you the most during your internship?**

The most rewarding aspect of my internship was the opportunity to participate in the development of a real-world software project. Working with technologies such as AWS, React, Spring Boot, and Google OAuth allowed me to gain practical experience that cannot be obtained through classroom learning alone.

**What do you think could be improved for future interns?**

I believe the internship program could be enhanced by organizing more technical workshops, knowledge-sharing sessions, or regular discussions between mentors and interns. These activities would provide additional opportunities to exchange ideas, learn from experienced engineers, and better understand industry practices.

**Would you recommend this internship program to your friends? Why?**

Yes, I would definitely recommend this internship program. It offers valuable hands-on experience, exposure to modern technologies, and opportunities to work on real projects while receiving guidance from knowledgeable mentors. It is an excellent environment for students who want to strengthen both their technical and professional skills before starting their careers.

* Increase opportunities for interaction between mentors and interns through technical discussions and team activities.

### Conclusion

This internship has been an invaluable learning experience that allowed me to apply my academic knowledge in a professional environment while developing both technical expertise and workplace skills. I sincerely appreciate the support and guidance provided by my mentor and the entire team throughout the internship. The experience and knowledge I gained will serve as a strong foundation for my future studies and professional career.
